About Me

I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology at the University of Sanya, and a remote research assistant working with Dr. Kyrie Zhixuan Zhou at the University of Texas at San Antonio. I am at the beginning of my research journey, and my current focus is the evaluation of LLM-based agents: how a benchmark should be designed so that it is genuinely difficult, separates strong models from weak ones, resists contamination and leaderboard overfitting, and can be reproduced by others.

Right now I am studying long-horizon engineering benchmarks, CAD-modeling tasks, and computer-use agents (CUA), with the goal of contributing to the design of new agent benchmarks. In the longer term, I am drawn to 3D vision, especially point clouds, and to the question of how agents can reason about and operate on 3D geometry.
