About

I'm a third-year undergraduate in Data Science and Big Data Technology, and I work remotely with Dr. Kyrie Zhixuan Zhou at UTSA. I'm just starting out in research.

Right now I mostly think about how to evaluate LLM agents well: what makes a benchmark task genuinely hard, whether its scores actually separate strong models from weak ones, and how to keep it reproducible and hard to game. I'm reading long-horizon engineering benchmarks, CAD-modeling tasks and computer-use agents along the way.

Longer term, I want to go deep on 3D vision, especially point clouds. That's why there's a point cloud spinning up there instead of a photo.

Interests: LLM agents · agent benchmarks · computer-use agents · CAD · point clouds
